Tyra ♥ Privacy Policy

For the Tyra’s Emotional Journey app for Android. Effective 26 October 2026.

Tyra collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the app keeps on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves three small things in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet:

ItemWhy
The name you give the heroSo the game can greet her by name next time
The furthest level reachedFor the Continue button
Whether the music is on or offSo your choice is remembered

These stay on the device. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for, collect or share any personal information, including names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • It does not use the internet. The app requests no network permission, and everything it needs, including its font and music, is built in.
  • It shows no advertising and contains no in-app purchases.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or third-party code that could collect data.
  • It requests no device permissions such as camera, microphone or location.

Children

Tyra’s Emotional Journey is made for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. A parent or guardian can remove everything the game has saved at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
