Rescue the feelings. ♥ Find your superpowers.

Tyra’s Emotional Journey is a hand-drawn adventure for children aged 5 and up. Ten feelings are trapped in ten little worlds. Rescue each one and it becomes a positive friend, and gives you a superpower.

Free. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.

See the game Google Play: coming soon

A game about big feelings

Everyone gets lonely, scared, angry or jealous sometimes. In this game those feelings are not enemies. They are characters who got stuck, and Tyra helps them out.

1. Meet a feeling

Each world is built around one hard feeling, such as loneliness, fear, anger or sadness.

2. Solve it with kindness

Puzzles are solved with the powers Tyra has already won, like Love, Courage and Calm.

3. Win a superpower

The rescued feeling turns positive, for example frustration into joy, or annoyance into silliness.

For parents and teachers

The game gives children a gentle way to name feelings, see that they can change, and practise answering them with kindness, courage and humour.

  • Free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases
  • No accounts, no tracking, and no internet connection at all. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Short levels that can be played on their own or together with a grown-up
  • Hand-drawn, calm, and free of violence

Tyra’s Emotional Journey is a game, not therapy or medical advice. If a child is struggling with their feelings, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. Read our privacy policy.

Made by kids. Unfiltered imagination.

This game was dreamed up and designed by two children, with their dad and a little help from AI doing the typing. Every character, feeling and superpower started as a child’s idea.

We believe the next generation will move from coders to imagineers: the people who dream up new worlds and, with AI, build them.

Tyra is our first world. We hope it shows what children can create when nobody tells them to filter their ideas.
